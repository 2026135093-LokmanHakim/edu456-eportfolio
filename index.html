My name is Lokman Hakim bin Yusoff, and I am from Marang, Terengganu. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Art Education at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Puncak Alam. Before continuing my degree, I completed a Diploma in Fine Art, which provided me with a foundation in artistic knowledge, creative expression, and visual arts.

My interests extend beyond traditional fine art. I am passionate about digital art, graphic design, photography, animation, 3D modelling, music, game development, and artificial intelligence. I enjoy exploring how different forms of art and technology can come together to create new possibilities for creativity, communication, and education.

As a future art educator, my ambition is not only to teach but also to contribute to the growth and recognition of art in Malaysia. I believe that art plays an important role in shaping culture, preserving identity, and inspiring future generations. Through education and creative innovation, I hope to encourage greater appreciation for the arts and help strengthen their place in Malaysian society.

Through this EDU456 E-Portfolio, I aim to explore the potential of artificial intelligence as a tool for creativity and education while developing new skills that will support my journey as an artist and future educator.
